{# ------------------------------------------------------------ Groups #}

Groups

Register the groups your identity provider sends in the claim. Members of an Admin group are administrators: they see every scan and all settings. Members of a User group can run scans, see their own and shared scans, and keep agent tokens. If someone is in several groups the highest role wins. Names must match exactly (upper and lower case count). Changes apply to everyone on their next request.

Anyone who can sign in at the identity provider currently gets User access, whatever their groups. Choose Denied under "Unlisted accounts" to let in registered groups only.
