Access

Sign people in with your identity provider (OpenID Connect) and decide, from their groups, who is an administrator and who may use SpiderFoot. Every scan then belongs to the person who started it and can be shared with users or groups.

The password file still has accounts. They keep working for scripts and the command line client with HTTP Basic authentication and have administrator rights.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------ Roles #}

Groups and roles

One group name per line (or separated by commas). Members of an admin group are administrators and see every scan and all settings. Members of a user group (or anybody, when the user groups list is empty) can run scans, see their own and shared scans, and keep their agent tokens. Anyone else who signs in is turned away.
