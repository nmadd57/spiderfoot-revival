Relevance

A common name pulls in namesakes, and every namesake fans out into more records. Relevance scoring rates each finding of a person or business-name scan for “is this about my target” from local rules (name and middle-name comparison, places, shared identifiers, which finding it was found under), then optionally from embedding similarity and an AI model for the borderline ones. Nothing is deleted: unrelated findings are hidden behind a counter and can be shown and corrected on the scan page.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------ scoring #}

Scoring

Pruning stops clearly unrelated people, companies and accounts from being followed up by other modules. It only applies to person and business-name scans, never to domains or IP addresses.

Between “hide below” and “likely” a finding is labelled unclear and stays visible.

{# --------------------------------------------------------- embeddings #}

Embeddings (optional)

Similarity between a short description of the target and each borderline finding is one signal among several. By default the endpoint from Settings → Web Search is used; fill in the fields below to use a different one for relevance. Vectors are cached, so rerunning costs nothing.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ AI #}

AI judgement (optional)

Findings that are still unclear after the local rules are sent in small batches to the model configured under AI Assistant, which answers same / different / unknown for each. Identical findings are asked once. Failures leave the local score in place.
